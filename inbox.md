# Inbox

Fast capture. Add new items to the **bottom** of the list — that way you can
open this file anywhere, jump to the end, and paste without thinking about
where it goes.

Format:

```markdown
- [ ] <link> — why it caught your eye
```

The note after the dash matters more than the link. "Tailscale subnet routing"
means nothing in three months; "might let me reach the dev boxes from the road"
still does.

Tick an item off when it's been promoted to a file in `ideas/` or consciously
dropped, and say which. Ticked lines can be deleted whenever the list gets
noisy — anything worth keeping is in `ideas/` by then.
